# PAL marketing direction

## Current decision

PAL is prioritising practical AI workflows, shared frameworks and community learning. Course sales are paused. The course catalogue at `/courses` is accessible directly, but is not in the header or footer navigation. Codex is listed as archived at `/course/codex-masterclass`, with no registration or embedded waitlist.

The previous SceneShift masterclass ad is a superseded case-study asset. Keep the file as production history; do not use its course/waitlist CTA for the current campaign.

## What the next video should do

Demonstrate one useful PAL method so people understand the value before deciding to engage. Start with professionals or solo founders who already use AI but struggle to get consistent, useful results.

- Hook: “AI gave you an answer. Can you actually use it?”
- Demonstration: give AI the same sample meeting notes twice. First ask for a generic summary; then provide a structured brief asking for decisions, next actions, owners and unresolved questions. Do not invent missing owners or deadlines.
- Payoff: show the real outputs and point out the practical difference. Label the notes as a sample. Do not invent time-saved or productivity statistics.
- Takeaway: goal → context → constraints → success criteria → review.
- CTA: “Explore PAL's frameworks”, linking to the existing `/periodic-table` resource. There is no course, signup or paid offer in this cut.

SceneShift's role is business brief → audience angle → scene/script plan → video render → review → revision. QwenWork authors the plan; the rendering tools turn it into a file. The proposed demonstration has not yet been produced.

## What we want to learn

1. Positioning: can viewers explain what PAL helps them do?
2. Relevance: do target viewers recognise their own work problem?
3. Intent: do they explore the framework or ask about applying it to their work?
4. Production: can we make a brand-appropriate video and improve it from feedback without starting over?

For the first small test, show the cut to 5–10 target viewers and ask what PAL does and what they would try next. These are proposed research actions, not completed results. If sharing the video publicly later, compare two hooks with the same demonstration and CTA. Use completion/retention where the channel provides it, tagged website visits where analytics is available, and relevant use-case replies. Views alone do not establish demand.

The next commercial offer should follow repeated audience needs. It is intentionally undecided while we collect that evidence.
