# prompt-alchemist-intro
Official intro / marketing site for Prompt Alchemist.

The free resource library is at `/resource`, with individual resources at `/resource/<resource-id>`. It is intentionally absent from the header and footer menus. The first resource, **Show AI what you mean**, is at `/resource/prompting-basics`: a five-page visual guide to zero-shot, one-shot and few-shot prompting, with a swipeable reader, copyable prompts and direct PDF/ZIP downloads. No account or form is required. See [resource maintenance](docs/resources.md) to add more resources.

The course catalogue is at `/courses`, with individual outlines at `/course/<course-name>`. It is intentionally absent from the header and footer menus. Codex Masterclass is listed as archived at `/course/codex-masterclass`; the old `/masterclass/codex` URL redirects there. Registration and the waitlist embed are absent from the archived page.

The original course source and assets remain preserved in [the source archive](archive/codex-masterclass/README.md). The current campaign direction is documented in [PAL marketing direction](docs/marketing-direction.md).
